FINANCE DEPARTMENT - EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
==================================================

1. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
   The following work-related expenses are eligible for reimbursement:
   - Travel (flights, trains, buses, taxis for official duty)
   - Accommodation during official outstation visits
   - Meals during official travel (up to Rs. 500/day domestic, Rs. 1500/day international)
   - Office supplies purchased with prior approval
   - Client entertainment (with manager pre-approval and receipts)

2. NON-ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
   - Personal travel or leisure activities
   - Alcohol purchases
   - Fines or penalties
   - Expenses without supporting receipts

3. SUBMISSION PROCESS
   - Submit the Expense Claim Form within 30 days of incurring the expense.
   - Attach original receipts or digital scans for all items above Rs. 200.
   - Get your Line Manager's sign-off before submitting to Finance.
   - Submit via: Finance Portal (https://finance.internal.gov/claims) or drop at Finance Desk, Room 204.

4. REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
   - Approved claims are processed within 7 working days.
   - Payments are made via direct bank transfer to your registered account.
   - Check claim status on the Finance Portal under "My Claims".

5. TRAVEL ADVANCE
   - Employees may request a travel advance of up to 80% of estimated travel cost.
   - Advance must be settled within 7 days of return with receipts and unused cash.
   - Repeat advance requests will not be processed if previous advance is unsettled.

6. AUDIT & COMPLIANCE
   - All expense claims are subject to random audit by the Finance team.
   - Fraudulent claims will result in disciplinary action and recovery of amounts.

Contact: finance@orin.gov | Ext. 1300 | Room 204
