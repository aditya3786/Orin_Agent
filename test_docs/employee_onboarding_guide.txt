EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
==========================

Welcome to the organization! This guide will help you get set up in your first week.

DAY 1 - ARRIVAL & SETUP
  - Report to Reception at 9:00 AM with your offer letter and ID proof.
  - Collect your ID card, access card, and welcome kit from HR (Room 101).
  - IT will set up your laptop, email account, and system access by noon.
  - Your email format: firstname.lastname@orin.gov
  - Attend the Orientation Session at 2:00 PM in Conference Hall A.

WEEK 1 CHECKLIST
  [ ] Complete HR documentation (Form 16, bank details, emergency contact)
  [ ] Set up MFA on your office account (IT will assist)
  [ ] Read and sign the IT Acceptable Use Policy
  [ ] Complete mandatory online training modules on the Learning Portal
  [ ] Meet your assigned buddy/mentor
  [ ] Attend department introduction with your team lead

IMPORTANT PORTALS & CONTACTS
  - HR Portal:       https://hr.internal.gov          (Leave, payslips, policies)
  - IT Portal:       https://it.internal.gov          (Support tickets, equipment)
  - Finance Portal:  https://finance.internal.gov     (Expense claims, advances)
  - Learning Portal: https://learn.internal.gov       (Training courses)

  - HR Helpdesk:      hr@orin.gov          | Ext. 1200
  - IT Helpdesk:      it-support@orin.gov  | Ext. 1100
  - Finance:          finance@orin.gov     | Ext. 1300
  - Your HR Partner:  onboarding@orin.gov  | Ext. 1205

BENEFITS OVERVIEW
  - Health Insurance: Family floater plan (self + spouse + 2 children). Enroll within 30 days of joining.
  - Provident Fund: 12% of basic salary contributed by employee; matched by employer.
  - Annual Bonus: Performance-based, paid in Q1 of the following year.
  - Learning Budget: Rs. 25,000/year for approved courses and certifications.

We're glad to have you on board. Reach out to HR any time — we're here to help!
