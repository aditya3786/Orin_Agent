IT DEPARTMENT - EQUIPMENT REQUEST & SUPPORT PROCEDURES
=======================================================

1. REQUESTING NEW EQUIPMENT
   - Submit an Equipment Request Form via the IT portal (https://it.internal.gov/request).
   - Include justification, required specifications, and approval from your Department Head.
   - Standard delivery time: 5–7 working days for in-stock items; up to 21 days for special orders.
   - Approved equipment categories: Laptops, Monitors, Keyboards, Mice, Headsets, Webcams.

2. IT SUPPORT & HELPDESK
   - Raise a support ticket at https://it.internal.gov/helpdesk or call ext. 1100.
   - Priority levels:
       P1 (Critical): System down, data breach — response within 1 hour.
       P2 (High): Major service disruption — response within 4 hours.
       P3 (Medium): Performance issues — response within 1 business day.
       P4 (Low): General queries — response within 3 business days.

3. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION
   - Only IT-approved software may be installed on office systems.
   - Submit a software request with business justification via the IT portal.
   - Approval required from IT Security before any new software is deployed.
   - Commonly approved tools: MS Office 365, Zoom, Slack, Adobe Acrobat.

4. PASSWORD & ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
   - Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
   - Minimum length: 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol.
   - Account lockout occurs after 5 failed login attempts — contact helpdesk to unlock.
   - Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access.

5. DATA BACKUP POLICY
   - All work data must be stored on the official network drive or SharePoint.
   - Local machine backups are performed automatically every night at 11 PM.
   - Employees are responsible for not storing sensitive data on personal devices.

Contact: it-support@orin.gov | Helpdesk: ext. 1100
