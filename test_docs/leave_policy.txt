LEAVE POLICY - HUMAN RESOURCES DEPARTMENT
==========================================

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
   - All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
   - Leave must be applied for at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal.
   - Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days to the next year.

2. SICK LEAVE
   - Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
   - Medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
   - Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

3. MATERNITY / PATERNITY LEAVE
   - Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children.
   - Paternity leave: 15 days of paid leave within 6 months of childbirth.

4. EMERGENCY / CASUAL LEAVE
   - Employees are entitled to 8 days of casual leave per year.
   - Casual leave cannot be clubbed with annual or sick leave.
   - Applications must be submitted through the departmental head.

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
   - Employees may apply for LWP when all other leave types are exhausted.
   - LWP approval is at the discretion of the Department Head and HR.

6. HOW TO APPLY
   - Log in to the HR portal at https://hr.internal.gov
   - Navigate to Leave Management > Apply Leave
   - Select leave type, dates, and reason
   - Submit for approval — you will receive an email confirmation within 24 hours

Contact HR at hr@orin.gov or call ext. 1200 for queries.
